# GCP-Follow-On
A web application detailing my experience, challenges, and overall takeaways from traveling in Greece through Ohio University's GCP program. Sponsored by the Gilman Scholarship Program in relation to their Follow-On Service Project. 

Concept: Website to showcase my travels and experience abroad. Timeline detailing my experience from application to in-country. Highlighting resources for financially disparaged students who may be supporting themselves on the journey.
